# CAIRN FC — 14-Player Squad, 11v11 Coaching & Carbon Light Transfers

## Overview of Latest Enhancements

In response to feedback, we have implemented three critical improvements across **CAIRN FC — AI Football Championship Manager**:

1. **Purged All '5 a side' / '5 agents' References**:
   - Replaced all legacy references with full **11-player squad** / **11v11 squad** terminology.
   - The **Squad Tactical Studio** now specifies: `"Define your club identity & coach your full 11v11 squad in plain English"`.
   - Updated the Tactical Studio styling to the restrained **IBM Carbon Light Theme** with crisp 1px borders, `#FFFFFF` surfaces, and `#0F6B45` buttons.

2. **Main Front Page Lists All 14 Squad Players**:
   - Slicing to 5 players on the dashboard was eliminated.
   - The **Squad Overview** table on the main Dashboard now lists all **14 registered players** (11 Starters + 3 Substitutes).
   - Each row features the player's cartoon avatar portrait, jersey number, name, personality trait, **Status badge** (`STARTER` in light green or `BENCH` in light amber/layer), position role, OVR score, form indicator bars, market valuation, contract year, and an **"Inspect →"** action button opening the detailed Player Dossier.

3. **Complete Color Scheme Overhaul for the Transfers Page**:
   - Completely eliminated all black/navy dark backgrounds (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.95)`, `bg-slate-900`, etc.) and all low-contrast dark-on-dark text.
   - Transformed the page into pure **IBM Carbon Light Theme**:
     - **Financial & Scouting HUD**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` card, 1px `#E0E0E0` border, `#161616` primary typography, `#0F6B45` for available funds, `#0F62FE` for squad value, and `#525252` subtitles.
     - **Navigation Tabs & Tier Pills**: High-contrast light pills (`#DEFBE6` background with `#0F6B45` text when active; `#FFFFFF` with `#E0E0E0` border when inactive).
     - **Search Bar**: White background `#FFFFFF` with 1px `#E0E0E0` border and `#161616` text.
     - **Transfer Target Cards**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface cards with 1px `#E0E0E0` borders, `#161616` player names, `#525252` club names, and high-contrast status bars:
       - Refuses / Unrealistic: Soft `#FFF1F0` background with `#DA1E28` text and `#FFD8D8` border.
       - Doubtful: Soft `#FEF7E0` background with `#8E6A00` text and `#F1C21B` border.
       - Interested / Available: Soft `#DEFBE6` background with `#0F6B45` text and `#A7F0BA` border.
     - **Action Buttons**: Carbon primary green `#0F6B45` for Sign buttons; soft `#FEF7E0` for Over Budget; soft `#FFF1F0` for Refuses.
     - **Chief Scout Dossier Modal**: Clean `#FFFFFF` modal with high-contrast text and light trait/verdict callout boxes.
